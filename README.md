# Car Kenya
This is where you find multiple types of cars in one place.For instance you want to buy a family sedan,you just go to the search bar and write the name let's say like Corola Axio, it brings you a variety of models,the year manufactured and the units available.
### Features
The best thing about our web application is based here,on the search bar when you want a specific car you just type in the words and it gives you all details about it eg the manufacturer year of the car,the condition of the car,the color,the importation cost of the car and the price of the car.
## Problem
Nowarday there alot of car sellers anywhere you go.Not all car sellers are genuine,some are scammers,some sell's you a stolen vehicles and others sell the cars at a higher prices.
### Solution
we have come with this brilliant web application to help those who want to buy a car at genuine prices,a car in good condition and the legitimacy ownership of the vehicle.
